## Required adapter boundary

Prefer a native, complete-interval joint-placement certificate first. If insufficient, reconstruct native per-ID geometry with declared row/path/pitch/face-writing strategies; keep source text, chronology, attachments, complete glyph coverage and explicit sizes. Do not shrink the entire rendered effect into a rectangle. Preserve off/manual/lock/emphasis priority and typed unsupported or insufficient-space diagnostics.

Record complete owned ink and plates before clipping, then evaluate the actual clipped stage ink with clip ownership and painter order preserved. Source-internal intentional clip/reveal does not permit avoidance-created missing glyphs. MainDraw return boxes omit many `env.draw`, direct Canvas, plates and moving fragments, so they cannot certify the whole composition. Apply original stage → local affine/projective-proxy → camera/FX exactly once, with all ghost passes and real/motion clocks. Full-interval proofs need analytic envelopes or verified finite-state partitions at every cull/schedule/modulo/step change; isolated time samples do not prove them. Degenerate/near-singular matrices and perspective denominators require explicit capability handling.

Sampler capability must distinguish primary source raster space from stage ink, include font/metric revision and sampler parameters in provenance/cache, and never transform the sampling canvas as if it were stage decoration. Native source sampling failure/empty coverage cannot become safe success. Clip capability must retain matrix-at-clip creation, union/intersection topology and evenodd holes; moving the text without its clip/plate breaks intent. Blur/shadow require finite alpha-threshold support or unsupported diagnostics.
