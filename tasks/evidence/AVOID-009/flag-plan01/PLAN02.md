## Source facts and hard constraints

These are native spatial tessellation nodes, not temporal proof samples. The shading/trims use one nonrectangular Canvas clip, closed with straight segments. Wf=.62W (landscape) or .78W (portrait); Hf=min(.52H/.34H,.64Wf). Pole rectangle is 1.2H long. At 960x540: Wf595.2,Hf280.8,poleX153.6,baseY113.4,poleTop29.16,poleBottom677.16. Physical pole length and raise/lower travel recipes are required, not whole-effect/canvas shrinking.

zf uses pow(u,.85). The finite difference at u=0 evaluates zf(-.01), which is NaN; early unfurl nodes also hit this domain and can propagate NaN to xAcc. This is a normal numeric source limitation, not a geometry certificate. OFF must remain unchanged, including its existing behavior; retain native failure evidence. Proposed continuation: evaluate zf at max(0,u) for endpoint finite differences only, retaining zf(u) exactly for physical u>=0, N=30, the original .01 difference span, phases, wave terms, wind, and all painter order. Record this changed derivative rule explicitly; it is not native pixel parity.

Original source reveal is `if (uu > ext) continue`, with uu=uC+side*g.x/Wf and ext=max(.001,unf*(1-furl)). Positive stagger can prevent apparent entry overhang; stagger=0 removes that delay. ## Continuous proof approach

Bound native ext in [.001,1], unf/furl in [0,1], fixed numeric wind/motion eligibility and all realTime/motionTime ghost clocks. Amax=Hf*.1*wind*(.5+.7motion)*2.2. For material u in [0,1], |z|<=Amax*(u^.85+.35u). Endpoint derivative extension additionally uses u<=1.01. Prove finite dz from a Holder bound for u^.85 plus sine Lipschitz bounds; retain native .02Wf denominator. Then cs>=1/sqrt(1+dzMax²)>0. Prove hs=1+z/(1.3Wf)>0 or return typed unsupported.

Each native xAcc increment is Wf*ext/N /sqrt(1+dz²), giving positive lower/upper bounds. Interpolated x, top/bot, sl and hs are convex combinations of adjacent source nodes. Bound actual recorder polygon/line/ellipse guards, not just physical surface. Text affine shear uses a second finite difference and denominator abs(deltaX)+.001; use monotone xAcc lower bounds and the glyph material margin to prove useful finite slope bounds. If needed partition at native clamp/visibility cases and floor(N*uu/ext) changes; interval trig extrema and bounded interval refinement must enclose every branch, not a sampled frame union. Inconclusive or exhausted interval budget is typed unsupported.

Build owner envelopes for cloth/trim/shading, pole+finial, rings, and every complete glyph, including font/stroke/font-space/device guards and analytic camera/ghost support. All receive the SAME fixed segment translation so cloth/pole/rings/glyphs stay attached. Render validates final seal, scale .1..4, exact active circle and stage, and actual event containment in analytic envelopes; no render-time repair. Full-reveal physical attachment uses the actual concave cloth domain/guarded outline, not just its convex hull or glyph center. Declared native entry overhang remains in the full owned union, never excluded from safety tests.
