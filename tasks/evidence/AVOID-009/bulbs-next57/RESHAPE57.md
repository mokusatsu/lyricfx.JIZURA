## Readability and clearance proposals

The contract's adaptiveLayoutsA primary max(18,.035*shortSide) and secondary max(12,.02*shortSide), and its .95 through .70 scales, are useful comparison anchors only. They are NOT an existing D policy. This proposal explicitly adopts those absolute floors for this pilot, adds a much stronger local primary floor .70*129.6=90.72, and preserves the original secondary20.736. At540, the absolute primary anchor18.9 and secondary12 are weaker than these retained values. Explicit user font-size/contentScale requests prohibit the reduced-font family; do not reinterpret an explicit size as adjustable.

Proposed held readability targets, to be checked against actual glyph/stroke/AA support rather than nominal advances:

- Primary glyph ink-to-outline gap at least max(2,.025*129.6)=3.24 stage px; main rows have at least max(2,.04*localPrimarySize) clear ink gap. Source lead remains1.12; reject a font/field failing that gap rather than changing tracking or squeezing glyphs.
- Main/caption ink gap at least max(3,.25*20.736)=5.184 px. Caption keeps its complete original source/truncation result, family, tracking .18, actual size trajectory and fade; if that text at original source size cannot fit, reject the field. No new ellipsis or caption downsize.
- Held bulb-body to main/caption ink gap at least max(2,.04*129.6)=5.184 px; no new held halo/ink envelope intersection, testing ONLY actual source-painted halos for the current N/i/lit predicate (no fictitious constant halo on an unlit bulb). This is a conservative scene-quality target, not a pre-existing ban on decorative overlap. Halo overlap is recorded separately and may require an explicit later aesthetic decision, never a silent omitted owner.
- Held neighboring bulb bodies retain a clear chord gap at least .5*9.72=4.86 px. Measure the actual last-to-first neighbor too, at every held source N. This target retains a visible dark interval, though it is less than the native nominal gap (3.3r-2r=1.3r). It is a proposed quality threshold, not a mathematical consequence of the original source.

These new quality numbers need parent design approval and human MP4 review. They do not establish raster safety. Moving-entry/exit packing gaps and overlaps are RECORDED aesthetic diagnostics, not hard acceptance inherited from the original same-N/q reference: compacting the rim necessarily changes those gaps. Preserve every source paint and show the original and reshaped pop in the human MP4. Held quality gaps, actual painted halo/ink clearance and no-owner-loss remain hard pilot criteria. Circle/stage obligations remain independently checked on every positive source paint, using the explicit source-moving exemptions actually authorized for the diagnostic, not a blanket exemption for the whole sign.
