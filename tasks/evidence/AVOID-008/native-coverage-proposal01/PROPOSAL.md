## Hanging tag: finite implementation proposal

Keep source W/H, params.variant='hang', all actual source strings/occurrences/fonts/painter order and every anchor/string/paper/shadow/hole/strike. Mark fixed anchor separately and tag all transformed events as one native hanging body with its exact current pivot, angle/drop and local frame. Fixed anchor is strict whenever drawn; a later main/price entry must not relax it. main retains noHold:true, plain:true and miAt(env,.2); pa remains tin(.35,.35,outBack)*tout, clamped at the original draw sites. Former-price strike across former-price is intentional and must not become a generic no-line/text-overlap failure. Current-price source growth from zero is intentional: test full-size held readability floor separately from native growth clocks.

Let S=params.swing, t=max(0,lt), z=inCubic(pOut), b=ltb. Exact native angle in degrees is

`theta=S*((1-z)*exp(-2.2*t)*cos(5.2*t)+1.5*z)+1.2*sin(1.1*b)`.

It never becomes static. For the original planned domain S in[-18,-10]union[10,18], t>=0,z in[0,1], the convex combination gives signed S*[-1,1.5]+[-1.2,1.2], conservatively abs(theta)<=28.2deg. In held z=0,t>=h, abs(theta)<=abs(S)*exp(-2.2*h)+1.2; h=.7 gives about5.06deg at S18, not zero. Apply ghost/quantized motion clocks, including their lower t bound, rather than substituting realTime for lt/ltb. For tighter bounded subintervals, damped-cos extrema solve tan(5.2*t)=-2.2/5.2 plus endpoints, sine extrema are exact; interval multiplication with z retains all Cartesian combinations. Full broad bound is already a valid fallback, avoiding root-finding for the complete mixed derivative.

Entry e=clamp(lt/.45), drop=-.4H*(1-outBack(e,1.4)). Native outBack overshoot is exactly B=4*1.4^3/(27*2.4^2), so drop is within[-.4H,+.4H*B] (approximately[-.4H,+.02824H]); it is zero after.45. Exit changes angle, not this drop formula. Invert each event's actual recorded rigid transform, including drop and local shadow offsets, to recover the native full-size support; sweep local vertices over exact trig endpoint/extremum intervals, independently combine x/y intervals with **all four rectangle corners**, then apply effective style/FX/contentScale/camera/ghost envelope. Main raw glyph ink, line half-width/miter/AA, hole and shadow are included. Full old/new price text and maximum clamped growth cover reveal subsets; a single early record may miss zero-alpha later events, so record at .7+epsilon and prove earlier topology/subsets with per-owner clocks.

Finite candidate order: native joint translation first; then six physical-tag sizes .95,.9,.85,.8,.75,.7, with original source tw/th/top/ch/string-loop functions recomputed from physical tw (stage and native cord/anchor clocks remain fixed), original S and angle functions unchanged. These are tag-object dimensions, not a smaller viewport. All body/main/metadata retain exact logical copies; main>=.7 native and max(18,.035U) after contentScale; metadata>=.7 native and its actual native-capped secondary floor. Do not scale stage U globally: source shadow/hole/stroke dimensions need explicit source-owned treatment and physical lower bounds. Reject field overflow rather than dropping/squeezing metadata. Explicit size: preserve points and skip reducing recipes; native translation remains available. First finite success wins; exhausted valid candidates produce typed insufficient-space, invalid source/unsupported FX remain unsupported. ## Shadow: filter support and fixed-paper feasibility are separate

Native near=outCubic(clamp(lt/.9)) controls x=W/2+(1-near)*.03W, sx=sy=lerp(1.06,1,near), and blur=allowFilter?lerp(.08*size,.012*size,near):0. near reaches1 at.9; entry alpha gate uses tin(0,.3,outCubic), exit source gate uses1-clamp((pOut-.5)/.5), glow=.92+.08*noise1(.8*ltb,seed). Do not invent a shared item alpha=a: mainDraw does not explicitly receive that a, and its actual native alpha/painter state must be recorded. After.9 silhouette geometry/blur is fixed, paper luminance still changes. Paper/lattice positions are static from their first visible source draw and remain strict throughout other events' entry.

Proposed shared primitive: an explicit sealed-C, named-source bounded **item blur** capability at09a item interception/geometry. Preserve the original item (including blur) in replay args and call stock J.drawItem with the actual allowFilter flag. Unblurred glyph geometry may be computed internally only as the seed for a proven filter-expanded support; never replay a blur-stripped item. Gate finite positive blur, allowed source/role, known stock implementation, no per-character blur, shadows, pieceFn, patterns or arbitrary ctx.filter strings. Both allowFilter true/false must be represented; false is the genuine native no-filter path, not substitute evidence for true. Leave all other recorder rejections in place.

A blur radius multiplier guessed from09j is insufficient. 03_text64–103 uses native drawItemLayered: glyph layout bounds, pad=2.6*blur+.12*size+stroke, k from current transform, ceil dimensions, reusable layer and native filtered drawImage; area>1.6*stage can instead use direct drawing. The **2.6 pad is neither a certified Gaussian support nor destination filter bound**. Geometry must account for both branches, their exact offscreen clipping and .1px filter-string rounding, render scale.1..4/AA/transforms, alpha>=1/255 and compositing alpha. Establish a documented bounded kernel/tail certificate for the supported browser/filter implementation (including any approximation/rounding), or retain typed unsupported-filter-support. Raster samples can falsify this bound but cannot establish a continuous kernel theorem. A Gaussian tail inequality is usable only with an established relation between actual Canvas blur and that kernel; do not label an arbitrary6-sigma box a portable proof. Broad support must be unioned before fixed foreground lattice occlusion so unproven occlusion cannot hide potential primary ink. No full-stage/per-glyph new raster buffer should be introduced; native drawItemLayered's existing layer may be replayed, with buffer lifetime/cost measured separately.

With today's ownership/topology, the held paper rectangle alone covers the whole stage. A fullstage-contained joint translation of that unchanged rectangle cannot avoid any active interior circle (even before adding wash/lattice/main/camera). Native camera crop can change the exact stage scope but does not remove held paper's circle intersection. This is a concrete source obstruction, not a font/filter failure. Moving only silhouette or calling paper independentDecor would evade the contract. Therefore a typed **measured** insufficient result can legitimately cover the unchanged full-stage shadow composition after true blur support is proven; it must include the actual owned record/proof and native candidate attempt, not return insuff before measuring the source.

Bounded strategies to consider without silently changing the variant: (1) native joint translation; (2) source-local silhouette x/y placement inside the same fixed architecture, with exact original glyph copy/font/near/blur clocks and no-new-lattice-occlusion proof. Under all-primary paper, (2) still fails the paper-circle necessary condition, so record that specific rejection rather than doing an expensive blind grid search. These actual source strategies cannot make the full fixed paper safe. An optional new **physical framed-shoji construction** could explicitly reframe every panel, wash, full original nP/cols/rows and complete silhouette into source-owned frame dimensions with native .7 point/object floors and joint translation; this is a product-design extension requiring parent scope/intent review, not an already valid generic viewport-scale candidate. A finite family (native plus six physical sizes) may test small corner circles but central full paper remains restrictive. Narrower/aspect-changing frame proposals would need per-cell physical/readability/complete-filter/lattice occlusion correspondence and human composition review. They cannot be asserted to preserve the original full fixed architectural geometry. No such new construction is implemented or approved here. If fixed full-stage geometry is mandatory, source-proven insufficient is the correct result; preserve unsupported until filter proof exists. Do not claim the whole shoji ID or all redesigned shadow constructions mathematically impossible.
