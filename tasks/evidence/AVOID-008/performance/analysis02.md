## Improvement proposals after all34 are fixed

1. Hoist immutable native semantic mapping/metadata/shape signatures once per cut; every candidate still compares the same ordered occurrences, fonts, clocks and owned shapes. Retain per-candidate owned writing-field/AA checks. Do not infer equivalence merely from same lyric/seed: these three clappers emit different SCENE and DATE metadata from line/start.
2. First retain the existing per-cut candidate cache and candidate order. Then consider bounded cross-finalization reuse for **exact keys only**: original normalized source cut including line/start/render/display/end/params/palette/explicit-size/effects, full plan stage/FX/style/events/beats/fps/region/evaluation inputs, query/quantization/native clocks, exact recipe/order, real font bytes/assignments/fallback + metrics revision, registered source function identity, adapter identity/version, renderer.drawCut/makeEnv identities and certification algorithm revision. No rounding/dropping fields. Use bounded entries and serialized-byte budgets including keys, errors and ownership payloads; immutable outputs/copy-on-read and explicit hit/miss/eviction/bytes accounting. Clear or miss on every identity change. A memory-only WeakMap identity token is not a substitute for source provenance in persisted evidence.
3. Split reuse of geometry-only prepared native programs/envelopes from region-circle solver results. Reuse only after proving independence of the omitted region inputs; otherwise include them exactly. Keep region order, cut.seed and candidate trial order identical. Preserve negative outcomes with their exact dependency keys; never promote unsupported/insufficient into safe. Private caches may be cold-cleared once and warm through the same protocol on both sources; do not selectively prewarm candidate outside the workload.
4. For analytic sweeps, group events only when the complete native body transform/clock/model, owner, clip semantics, camera dependencies and AA reserve agree. Apply source clipping first. Partition the text/nativeText reserve20 from shape reserve10 exactly (current stage/entry nativeText uses10 whereas hold uses20; no silent harmonization). A shared transform of a polygon union can be analyzed using a group hull or shared trig extrema, but a hull may enlarge a bound and change feasibility/candidate selection. Initially compute/reuse common trig coefficients while retaining each event's original vertices/envelope output. A later body-union certificate needs containment and original outcome/selected recipe/offset equality or an explicitly reviewed behavior change; conservative enlargement is not automatically acceptable under the requested unchanged bounds. Keep body versus hinged upper stick distinct, continuous32-interval bound, entry shake/drop, native clipping, AA guards and source clocks unchanged.
5. Do not cache final trajectories across different absolute start/line values without a proved source-clock and metadata remapping. The repeated clapper data are not byte-identical inputs. Exact same-project repeated finalizations could benefit; the100 heterogeneous cuts themselves may not. Measure key construction/serialization/retention costs rather than assuming caching helps.

All proposals are unimplemented product changes. Acceptance remains 2bb baseline, full34 workload, same fonts/browser/warmups, planning p95≤2x and added render p95≤3ms, plus unchanged geometry/AA/source/negative/cache-budget obligations. No candidate budget/order, region reach, clock or success threshold is relaxed.

## Native font corpus: source cause and harness preparation

The old preparation constructs its environment with makeEnv. `A.record` itself creates a proxy and **rebuilds helpers through renderer.makeEnv(proxy,...,{ctx:proxy,placementRecording:true})** (`09a`134). Thus missing helper rebinding is not the demonstrated cause. Its `placementLayoutsC:false` disables nativeC34 and nativeB25 raw paint interception; fallback guards reject fill/stroke and nonzero letterSpacing. The old receipt has4 letterSpacing,24 fill,6 stroke diagnostics over34 sampled programs. This matches the false mode's guards at `09a`86,121,128,133, and is preserved as unsupported.

The separately prepared fixture now uses `placementLayoutsC:true`, `placementUnits:true`, `layoutCRecipe:null`, `placementTrajectory:null`, full-stage/area-null source cut. It asserts exact ctx/cut/native source/renderer identity and uses absolute=cut.start+local time at1.5/3.2; no forced pIn/pOut. `A.record` can then enable the finite raw-path and finite-pixel-letterSpacing handlers and capture nativeCBody tags. No recipe, movement offset or final trajectory is supplied. All34 IDs remain in the corpus, and any remaining unsupported program remains failure. This version has syntax/static validation only; it has **not** been executed.

This mode is the registered C native source transport used by certification, not a proof of pixel equality to OFF/placementLayoutsC:false. The source wrapper selects exactCMeasure on this flag (`11p`8/214), and layout-specific branches add ownership/instrumentation and in places choose C text paths (dictionary1297). Those may affect pixels, metadata or measurement; a null recipe does not globally prove flag-invariance. Future authorized native pixel carry must compare appropriate native C-on/no-recipe versus actual selected composition under matching fonts, clocks, source, camera and dimensions, preserving source full-frame bytes where required. Complete font cmap/emitted-copy qualification is also separate from field/circle/stage safety, per-event AA bounds, pixel carry, and all-time clocks. The new scope string explicitly prevents conflating them.

`analysis02-static.json` pins the new harness/source state and gzip fixture bytes without launching Chromium. Original diagnosis pins remain authoritative for its performance numbers. Formal preparation remains gated on stable34 + parent authorization + current runtime identity; no additional execution is authorized by this document.
